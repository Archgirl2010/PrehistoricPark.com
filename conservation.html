Conservation at Prehistoric Park

Protecting
the Past.

Our conservation programme is dedicated to maintaining healthy populations of prehistoric species and ensuring their continued survival for generations to come.

Our Mission

Conservation Through Care

Prehistoric Park exists to protect species that no longer have a natural place in the modern world. Our work combines animal welfare, population management, scientific research and habitat design.

Every animal in our care is monitored by dedicated zoological and veterinary teams. Individual health, behaviour and social relationships are recorded throughout the year to help us provide the highest possible standard of care.

Where appropriate, animals may participate in managed breeding programmes designed to maintain healthy and genetically diverse populations.

Population Management

Our teams carefully manage populations across the park to maintain genetic diversity and sustainable breeding groups.

Habitat Conservation

Habitats are designed to replicate important environmental features of the periods in which our animals evolved, while meeting modern standards for animal welfare and safety.

Veterinary Care

Specialist veterinary teams provide routine health assessments, preventative medicine and emergency care throughout the park.

Species Recovery

Prehistoric Park maintains long-term recovery programmes for selected species where population numbers, genetics or habitat requirements require additional attention.

Our Commitment

Every species matters. Every individual matters. Our responsibility is to ensure that the animals entrusted to our care continue to thrive.
