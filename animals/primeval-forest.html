Into the Primeval Forest

Primeval Forest is a densely planted habitat designed to recreate the atmosphere of an ancient prehistoric woodland. Tall trees, thick undergrowth, ferns and open forest clearings create a landscape where visibility is often limited.

The habitat contains a deliberately varied collection of prehistoric animals. Some are large herbivores, while others are powerful predators. Rather than displaying each animal separately, the environment allows visitors to experience the forest as a living ecosystem.

Not Everything Is Visible
Primeval Forest is designed around discovery. Animals may appear briefly between the trees before disappearing back into the vegetation. Take your time and watch the forest carefully.

A winding guest trail follows the outer sections of the habitat, with several protected viewing areas overlooking forest clearings and feeding grounds.

The Residents

Therizinosaurus

Therizinosaurus cheloniformis

An enormous herbivorous theropod famous for its exceptionally long claws. Despite its unusual appearance, it primarily fed on vegetation.

Deinocheirus

Deinocheirus mirificus

A massive, unusual dinosaur with a broad body, long arms and a distinctive duck-like bill. It spends much of its time near the forest's wetter clearings.

Dilophosaurus

Dilophosaurus wetherilli

An early Jurassic predator recognised by the paired crests on its skull. It occupies the more heavily wooded sections of the habitat.

Maip macrothorax

Maip macrothorax

A large megaraptoran predator from Patagonia. Its presence adds a particularly formidable predator to the forest environment.

Allosaurus

Allosaurus fragilis

A powerful Jurassic predator with a large skull, serrated teeth and distinctive ridges above the eyes. Allosaurus is one of the forest's dominant predators.

The Primeval Forest Experience

🌲 Forest Trail

Follow a winding path through dense prehistoric vegetation and shaded woodland.

🔭 Hidden Lookouts

Protected viewing platforms overlook clearings where animals frequently gather.

🌿 Forest Discovery

Take your time. The best sightings can happen when animals emerge unexpectedly from the vegetation.

Visitor Safety Primeval Forest contains large animals and active predators. Visitors must remain on designated trails and behind protective barriers. Do not leave the marked visitor route, enter restricted areas or attempt to attract the animals.
