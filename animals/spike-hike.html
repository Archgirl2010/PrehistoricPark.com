Walk Among the Armour

Spike Hike is a woodland and grassland habitat located beside Humble Herds. The landscape is dominated by ferns, cycads, low vegetation and mature trees, providing shade and shelter for the animals.

The habitat has been designed around the behaviour of armoured herbivores. Stegosaurs occupy the more open areas while the ankylosaurs spend much of their time among the lower vegetation and woodland margins.

Built to Withstand Them
The perimeter protection around Spike Hike is considerably stronger than the fencing used for the park's smaller herbivore habitats. Large animals with defensive tails, horns and armour require additional separation from visitors.

A quiet walking trail follows the outside of the habitat. Viewing areas have been positioned at several points along the route, allowing visitors to observe the animals without disturbing them.

Residents

Stegosaurus

Stegosaur

Easily recognised by its large back plates and powerful spiked tail. Stegosaurus is one of the most popular animals along the Spike Hike trail.

Chungkingosaurus

Stegosaur

A smaller Chinese stegosaur with paired plates and tail spikes. It is frequently seen browsing close to the woodland edge.

Wuerhosaurus

Stegosaur

A broad-bodied stegosaur adapted to feeding close to the ground. Its relatively low profile makes it particularly interesting to observe from the trail.

Ankylosaurus

Ankylosaur

A heavily armoured dinosaur equipped with a powerful tail club. Ankylosaurus tends to remain close to dense vegetation and shaded areas.

Stegouros

Ankylosaur

A smaller armoured dinosaur with a distinctive flattened tail structure. It occupies the lower woodland areas of the habitat.

Hylaeosaurus

Armoured Dinosaur

An early armoured dinosaur that uses the dense vegetation and woodland areas for cover.

The Spike Hike Experience

🌿 Woodland Trail

Follow the winding guest path through a landscape filled with ferns, cycads and prehistoric vegetation.

🦴 Armour Lookouts

Protected viewing areas provide close views of the animals while maintaining a safe distance.

🌳 Quiet Habitat

This section of the park is deliberately quieter, allowing visitors to observe animals at their own pace.

Visitor Safety Spike Hike contains heavily armoured animals with powerful tails and defensive structures. Visitors must remain behind designated barriers and on marked paths. Do not attempt to touch, feed or attract the animals.
