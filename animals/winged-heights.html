Winged Heights

Above the Park

Winged Heights is a vast enclosed aviary designed specifically for prehistoric flying reptiles. Its enormous height allows the animals to make sustained flights rather than simply moving between perches.

The habitat combines tall trees, rocky ledges, shallow water, open ground and elevated platforms. Visitors enter through a secure double-door system before following a winding trail beneath the flight space.

Look Up
The largest residents can have wingspans measured in metres. Visitors may see them gliding overhead, circling above the canopy or landing on the elevated structures throughout the aviary.

Because the animals are capable of three-dimensional movement, sightings can occur almost anywhere within the enclosure. Keep an eye on the canopy as well as the ground.

Residents

Quetzalcoatlus

Quetzalcoatlus northropi

One of the largest known flying animals. Its immense wingspan makes an overhead encounter one of the most impressive sights in the park.

Pteranodon

Pteranodon longiceps

A familiar Late Cretaceous pterosaur with a long crest and narrow beak. Often seen gliding above the central water area.

Tapejara

Tapejara wellnhoferi

A smaller crested pterosaur known for its distinctive head shape and highly manoeuvrable flight.

Dimorphodon

Dimorphodon macronyx

An early pterosaur with an unusually large head and relatively short wings. Frequently observed around the lower rock formations.

Kunpengopterus

Kunpengopterus antipollicatus

A small darwinopteran pterosaur nicknamed "Monkeydactyl", known for its unusual grasping adaptations.

The Winged Heights Experience

🦅 Flight Overhead

Walk directly beneath the flight space and watch the animals navigate the enormous aviary above you.

🌿 Canopy Trail

Follow the elevated trail through woodland and rocky viewing areas designed to bring visitors closer to the upper levels of the habitat.

💧 Watering Grounds

A large shallow water area attracts several species throughout the day and provides excellent viewing opportunities.

Visitor Safety Winged Heights is an enclosed flight habitat. Visitors must remain on designated paths and must not attempt to attract, feed or touch the animals. Do not throw objects into the aviary.
