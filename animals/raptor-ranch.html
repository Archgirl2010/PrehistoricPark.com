About the attraction

Raptor Ranch

Raptor Ranch is one of Prehistoric Park's most closely monitored predator attractions. Six separate habitats house a range of feathered theropods and dromaeosaurids, each occupying an enclosure designed around its size, behaviour and natural environment.

Unlike the enormous open landscapes of Giants of the Valley, Raptor Ranch is designed around close observation. Guests can watch these highly specialised predators from a large central viewing shelter surrounded by multiple secure habitats.

The habitat

Six Enclosures

The ranch contains six differently sized enclosures arranged around the visitor viewing area. Dense vegetation, rocky terrain, open ground and sheltered areas provide each species with an environment suited to its needs.

The largest habitats are designed for the more powerful predators, while smaller species occupy specialised environments containing climbing, perching and hiding opportunities.

Visitor experience

Watch From Above

Guests enter the ranch through a secure visitor shelter positioned between the habitats. Elevated viewing platforms allow visitors to observe the animals without entering their territory.

Interpretive displays explain the evolution of dromaeosaurids, their hunting strategies and the remarkable adaptations that made these predators so successful.

Meet the residents

The Raptors

Achillobator

Large Dromaeosaurid

A powerful Central Asian predator occupying one of the ranch's largest secure habitats.

Velociraptor

Small Dromaeosaurid

A highly agile predator whose speed and hunting behaviour make it one of the ranch's most closely watched residents.

Microraptor

Four-Winged Raptor

A small feathered dromaeosaurid adapted to an environment containing dense vegetation and elevated perches.

Dromaeosauroides

European Dromaeosaurid

A lesser-known raptor represented at the ranch as part of its wider collection of prehistoric predators.

Wulong

Feathered Dromaeosaurid

A small, lightly built predator from Early Cretaceous China with an especially striking feathered appearance.

Utahraptor

Giant Dromaeosaurid

One of the ranch's largest and most formidable residents, requiring a heavily reinforced enclosure.

Explore the ranch

The Raptor Experience

Central Viewing Shelter

A large protected visitor structure sits at the heart of Raptor Ranch, providing views across several habitats while keeping guests safely separated from the animals.

Predator Behaviour

Displays and observation stations explain hunting behaviour, communication, pack dynamics and the evolution of predatory dinosaurs.

Research & Conservation

Selected areas of the ranch are connected to the park's research and conservation programme, allowing staff to study behaviour and welfare away from public view.

Visitor Safety

Raptor Ranch is a high-security predator facility. Visitors must remain within designated viewing areas and follow all instructions from park staff. Guests are never permitted inside animal habitats.
