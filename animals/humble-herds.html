Where the Herds Gather

Humble Herds is a large mixed herbivore habitat located in the south-eastern section of Prehistoric Park. Open grasslands merge with patches of woodland, shallow streams and sheltered areas where the animals can retreat from view.

The habitat has been designed around natural herd behaviour. Different species share the wider landscape while dedicated sections provide feeding, veterinary and separation areas when required.

Watch the Herds
The animals are not placed on display individually. Visitors may see several species moving through the habitat together, creating one of the most active environments in the park.

A series of guest paths and elevated viewing platforms follow the perimeter of the habitat. Large electrified perimeter fencing provides separation between visitors and the animals while allowing wide views across the enclosure.

The Humble Herds Experience

🌾 Open Grasslands

Wide viewing areas overlook the main feeding grounds, providing visitors with long-distance views across the mixed herds.

🌳 Woodland Walk

Follow the perimeter trail through shaded woodland and discover smaller species moving through the vegetation.

🔭 Herd Lookout

Elevated platforms provide a higher viewpoint across the habitat and are particularly useful when animals are gathered farther from the main path.

Visitor Safety Humble Herds contains large free-moving animals. Visitors must remain behind designated barriers and should never attempt to feed, touch or attract the animals. Please remain on marked paths at all times.
