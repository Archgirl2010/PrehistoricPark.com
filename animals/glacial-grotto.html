Welcome to the Ice Age

Glacial Grotto recreates a cold, rugged environment inspired by the landscapes occupied by some of the world's most famous Ice Age animals.

The habitat combines rocky terrain, open tundra, frozen ground and sheltered woodland. Artificial snow and temperature-controlled areas allow the animals to experience conditions appropriate to their natural environments.

A World of Ice
During the coldest periods, much of the northern world was covered by enormous ice sheets. The animals living here evolved to survive in some of the harshest environments experienced by large terrestrial mammals.

Visitors follow a winding trail around the habitat, with protected viewing areas overlooking the open plains and rocky shelters.

The Residents

Woolly Mammoth

Mammuthus primigenius

One of the most recognisable animals of the Ice Age. Thick fur, a layer of body fat and enormous tusks helped the woolly mammoth survive in cold northern environments.

Woolly Rhinoceros

Coelodonta antiquitatis

A heavily built rhinoceros covered in thick hair. Its large horn was used for feeding and potentially for defence.

Smilodon

Smilodon

The famous sabre-toothed cat. Smilodon possessed enormous upper canine teeth and a powerful build adapted for ambushing large prey.

Irish Elk

Megaloceros giganteus

Despite its common name, this enormous deer was not closely related to modern elk. Its enormous antlers could span several metres.

The Glacial Grotto Experience

❄️ Frozen Plains

Wide open areas recreate the sparse vegetation and exposed terrain of Ice Age environments.

🦣 Mammoth Lookout

A raised viewing platform overlooks the main grazing area and provides broad views across the habitat.

🪨 Grotto Trail

Follow the trail through rocky shelters and colder sections of the habitat where the predators can retreat from view.

Visitor Safety Glacial Grotto contains large animals and active predators. Visitors must remain on designated paths and behind protective barriers. Please do not feed, touch or attempt to attract the animals.
