A Valley Built for Giants

Giants of the Valley is the largest habitat complex in Prehistoric Park. A broad prehistoric valley of woodland, open grassland and rocky terrain provides room for some of the largest animals in the collection to move freely.

Unlike most areas of the park, the valley is not surrounded by conventional perimeter fencing. Instead, the habitat is enclosed by steep natural rock formations and carefully engineered barriers integrated into the landscape.

The River Ride
The best way to experience Giants of the Valley is from the water. The park's river route travels deep into the habitat, providing visitors with multiple viewing opportunities while keeping a safe distance from the animals.

Guests may encounter animals at the water's edge, moving between forest clearings or crossing the valley itself. Sightings vary depending on animal movement and conditions, making every journey different.

Residents

Mamenchisaurus

Mamenchisaurus

One of the longest-necked dinosaurs known. Its enormous reach allows it to browse vegetation high above the valley floor.

Amargasaurus

Amargasaurus cazaui

A distinctive sauropod recognised by the tall spines running along its neck and back.

Patagotitan

Patagotitan mayorum

One of the largest dinosaurs represented at Prehistoric Park. Adults require an enormous amount of space and vegetation.

Giraffatitan

Giraffatitan brancai

A towering sauropod whose elevated browsing height makes it particularly impressive when viewed from the river.

Argentinosaurus

Argentinosaurus huinculensis

One of the heaviest dinosaurs known. Sightings of this enormous animal are among the most memorable experiences in the valley.

Apatosaurus

Apatosaurus

A heavily built sauropod with a long tail and neck, often seen moving slowly through the lower woodland.

Nigersaurus

Nigersaurus taqueti

A smaller, specialised sauropod with an unusual wide muzzle adapted for feeding close to the ground.

The Valley Experience

🚤 River Journey

Travel deep into the habitat aboard the park's river vessels. The route provides views unavailable from the main guest paths.

🌲 Valley Lookouts

Elevated viewing points overlook sections of the valley and provide long-distance views across the habitat.

🦕 Giant Sightings

Watch the animals interact naturally as they move between feeding grounds, woodland and the river.

Visitor Information The Giants of the Valley is a remote habitat. Allow additional time to reach the river boarding area. Children must remain with an adult and all visitors must remain within designated viewing areas.
