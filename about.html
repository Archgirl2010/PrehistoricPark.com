Prehistoric Park is a world-class wildlife sanctuary dedicated to the conservation, study and care of living prehistoric animals.

Our Story

A Different Kind of Wildlife Park

Established in 2017, Prehistoric Park was founded with a simple ambition: to bring the world's prehistoric past back to life and give visitors the opportunity to experience animals that disappeared from the Earth millions of years ago.

Today, the park is home to one of the world's most significant collections of living prehistoric species. Our habitats have been designed around the needs of each animal, providing space, enrichment and natural environments wherever possible.

Alongside our visitor facilities, Prehistoric Park operates dedicated research, conservation and animal care programmes. Scientists, veterinarians and animal specialists work throughout the park to better understand the animals in our care and support their long-term future.

Conservation

Our conservation programme focuses on maintaining healthy populations of prehistoric species and preserving the genetic diversity of animals in our care.

The park works closely with zoological, veterinary and scientific organisations around the world.

Research

Our research teams study prehistoric animal behaviour, biology, ecology and welfare.

Research is conducted alongside our animal care teams to ensure that every habitat is designed around the animals that live there.
